# Glossary

**Invite**: the birthday web page guests open from a WhatsApp link.
**Birthday Boy**: Avkrit, turning 1 on 4 Dec.
**Hosts**: Avkrit's great-grandparents, Mr. Ashok Saxena and Mrs. Suman Saxena, who send the Invite.
**Guest**: anyone who receives the Invite; mostly family, on phones.
**RSVP**: a Guest's reply saying whether they will attend.
**Wish**: a birthday message a Guest leaves for the Birthday Boy.
**Wishes Wall**: the public list of Wishes on the Invite.
